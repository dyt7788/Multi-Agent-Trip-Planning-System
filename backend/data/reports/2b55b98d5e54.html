  • Yellow Crane Tower

Tips

  • Best times to visit: before 9 AM or after 4 PM to avoid crowds
  • 3-5 PM is great for sunset photos
  • Night view is stunning from 7:00-10:00 PM
  • Avoid weekends and public holidays due to crowds
  • Enter from the west gate for less waiting time
