Highlights

  • Yellow Crane Tower (Huanghelou)
  • East Lake (Donghu)
  • Wuhan University
  • Hubei Provincial Museum
  • Wuhan Yangtze River Bridge
  • Jianghan Road Walking Street
  • Cycling along East Lake Greenway
  • Night cruise on the Yangtze River
  • Exploring Wuhan University's cherry blossoms (spring)
  • Attending a bell performance at Hubei Provincial Museum

Restaurants

  • Cai Lin Ji (for hot dry noodles)
  • Lao Tongcheng (for sanxian doupi)
  • Xu Sao Hutangfen (for rice noodle soup)
  • Hubu Alley snack stalls
  • Jianghan Road food vendors

Tips

  • Visit Yellow Crane Tower in the afternoon to avoid crowds
  • Use Wuhan's metro system for efficient transportation between districts
  • Try local breakfast ('zao guo') culture at Hubu Alley but avoid overpriced tourist traps
  • Book museum visits in advance (especially Hubei Provincial Museum, closed Mondays)
  • Carry both sun and rain protection as Wuhan weather can be unpredictable

Risk Notes
